## Goal
A hired Cashier is a uniformed R15 avatar who walks to the counter and checks out the queue; a hired Lot Worker walks to the pickup row and loads customer cars, and restocks sold spots from the bays. Staff get gently tired, rest at a campfire break area on the lot, and go back to work. No wages, ever.

## Decisions
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Roles that walk | Cashier and Lot Worker (one each, `maxCount` 1). Lot Manager: config and save slot only; hire, avatar and computer job come with CM2-G9 (it needs HQ 4). |
| Cashier | Spawns at its break-area rest point, walks to `NpcPath.staffPoint()` (16, −16.5) = the counter art's `CashierStand` (counter centre z −14, +2.5 behind), stands there anchored facing queue slot 1. It serves the customer in `CheckingOut` after `CashierCheckoutSeconds` (3) counted from the first tick it is on duty while that customer waits. Away (walking, resting): the N2 rule applies (player prompt; walk-out after queue patience). |
| Lot Worker jobs | One at a time, priority: (1) load the oldest uncarried order: walk to its pickup tree, claim it there, carry it to the car door, load; (2) restock a sold spot: walk to a bay holding that size, carry a tree (visual only) to the spot, place it in one atomic `stockFromBay`. Stock zones (G13) later replace "sold spots". |
| Order claim | `order.carriedBy = Staff.CarrierUserId` (0; Roblox UserIds are ≥ 1 live, Studio test players ≤ −1, shadows 999999999001/2). A player gets `AlreadyCarried`; reload clears it (`clearCarried`). Timer auto-load (`autoLoad`, `WorkerLoadSeconds` 15) is removed. |
| Restock source | Sold spots = runtime list `session.vacatedSpots` (newest last, cap 20) filled by `npcSettle`; not saved. Bay: lowest index with the spot's size and the sold species, else lowest index with that size. Placement ID prefix `staff:<revision>:<clock>`. Bay count drops only at placement. |
| Energy (PROVISIONAL, "gentle") | 0–100. While on shift (every state except `Resting`) −0.2/s; resting +2/s. Tired at ≤ 20, rested at 100. Cashier leaves its post when tired; Lot Worker finishes its current job first. Work ≈ 6.7 min, break ≈ 40 s plus walks. Energy never stops work by itself at 0. |
| Energy save | Director copies whole-number energy into `state.staffEnergy` every 15 s without marking dirty; it rides along with the next save (autosave ≤ 60 s). Changes only while the save is live (no offline change). |
| Break area | Square 8 × 8 at `state.breakArea {x, z, rotation}` (rotation 0/90/180/270, visual only). Trees may not stand in it (`KeepClear`); it must lie in the lot and clear all KeepClear boxes, decor fences and trees. Migration and new saves take the first free candidate. Part-built campfire with three log seats (small decor, PRODUCT_SPEC Art). NPCs pass through it (structure group). |
| Budget and performance | Staff separate from customers: ≤ 3 per plot (one per role), ≤ 18 per server; tick 0.25 s; stationary staff (`OnDuty`, `Idle`, `Resting`) have an anchored root. Soak: 24 load customers + 12 load staff ≥ 55 FPS. |
| Wages | None. No code path debits cash for staff after the one-time hire (headless test 9). |

3. **`src/shared/Staff.lua`** (requires GameConfig, Stocking):
   - `Staff.newEnergy(): {[string]: number}` all roles 100.
   - `Staff.stepEnergy(energy, dt, resting): number` → `clamp(energy ± rate · dt, 0, Max)`.
   - `Staff.isTired(e)` (`e <= TiredAt`), `Staff.isRested(e)` (`e >= RestedAt`).
   - `Staff.checkBreakArea(input: LayoutInput, x, z): (boolean, string?)`: non-finite → `InvalidBreakArea`; box outside the lot at `input.lotExpansion` (`minX < −LotHalfWidth`, `maxX > LotHalfWidth`, `maxZ > LotHalfDepth`, `minZ < −(LotHalfDepth + BackDepthPerLevel · level)`) → `OutsideLot`; strict overlap (`a.min < b.max and b.min < a.max` on both axes) with any `Placement.KeepClear` box, or `hitsBox` of a `DecorFences` circle (radius `DecorFenceRadius`) → `KeepClear`; any tree circle hitting the box → `TooClose`. `input.breakArea` is ignored.
   - `Staff.defaultBreakArea(state): {x, z, rotation}`: first candidate that passes `checkBreakArea(Stocking.layoutInput(state), …)`, else candidate 1; rotation 0; fresh table.
   - `Staff.toLot(area, offset): Point` with r = rad(rotation): `x = area.x + ox cos r + oz sin r`, `z = area.z − ox sin r + oz cos r` (rotation 90 maps (0, 3.6) to (3.6, 0)).
   - `Staff.restPoint(area, role)` = `toLot(area, RestPoints[index of role in Roles])`.
   - `Staff.pickupApproach(space): Point` = `{FirstX + Step · (clamp(space, 1, 4) − 1), ApproachZ}`; `Staff.bayApproach(bay): Point` = `{BayApproachX, −22 + 6 (bay − 1)}`.
