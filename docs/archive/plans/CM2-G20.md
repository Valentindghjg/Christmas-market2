## Goal
One command in a Studio Play session runs the whole manual MCP sequence of 2026-11-21 plus the open R1/G21 items and prints PASS/FAIL per step and a total. A separate real-time mode runs the literal 10-minute soak. Nothing new is reachable by clients or exists on live servers.
